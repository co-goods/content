# Digital

**Digital** describes information encoded as discrete values — bits — as distinct from *analog*, where a signal varies continuously. It is a property of how something is *represented*, not of whether it is made of matter: a digital file still needs a physical substrate (a disk, a wire, a chip) to exist.

This is why "physical versus digital" is a category error: digital is one kind of [[non-physical]] representation, so the clean opposite of [[physical]] is non-physical, with analog the true counterpart of digital. The [[physical-vs-digital]] comparison sets this out.
