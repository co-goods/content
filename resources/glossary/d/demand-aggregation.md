# Demand aggregation

The pattern of the platform winners that Aggregation Theory describes: own the demand relationship at scale and commoditize the supply side. It is powerful and real — and, in the co-goods reading, the **extractive** version of organising demand. Co-goods counter-positions toward [[demand-coordination]]: align the demand without capturing it, so value flows back rather than concentrating.
