# Moat

A **moat** is whatever lets a business sustain returns that competitors cannot easily erode — defensibility made structural. Warren Buffett popularised the "economic moat"; Hamilton Helmer's *7 Powers* turns it into a precise taxonomy of seven structural advantages (scale economies, network economies, counter-positioning, switching costs, branding, cornered resource, process power).

Of these, [[network-effects|network effects]] are among the strongest — which is why network-effects investors treat them as the defining moat of the digital economy. The co-goods reading is pointed: a moat built on antirivalness compounds value *for participants* as they join, rather than locking them in to extract from them — defensibility and shared benefit pulling in the same direction instead of against each other.
