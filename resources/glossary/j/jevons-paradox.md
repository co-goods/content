title: Jevons paradox
type: term
classes:
  - type: noun
    definitions:
      - The observation that improving the efficiency of using a resource can increase its total consumption rather than reduce it, because greater efficiency lowers cost and so raises demand. Named after William Stanley Jevons.
relevance: A caution for sustainability — efficiency alone can backfire; what changes the total is coordinating demand, not just making production leaner.
