# Sharing economy

Platform-mediated sharing of underused capacity — access over ownership. It's a useful precedent for co-goods: it shows how a platform can coordinate distributed resources and how shared access creates value. Olleros connects it directly to [[antirival-goods]] goods and [[network-effects|network effects]] — the dynamics co-goods aims to turn toward physical production and regeneration rather than extraction.
