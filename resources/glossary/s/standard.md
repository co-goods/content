# Standard

A documented agreement — a specification or set of rules — that lets independent parties interoperate and hold shared expectations. Standards are a [[coordination]] device: they let many actors align without negotiating from scratch each time.

Note the layers that share this word: *a standard* is also a **library content type** (a kind of catalogued work, like a book or paper — e.g. SPDX), and *standards* as a practice is a theme. This entry is the **concept**; specific standards are catalogued in the library and link back here.
