# Non-physical

**Non-physical** is the actual opposite of [[physical]]: everything that is not made of matter — ideas, knowledge, information, designs, relationships, software, brands. Such things are not used up when shared, which is what makes them candidates for [[nonrivalness|nonrival]] and [[antirivalness|antirival]] treatment.

[[digital|Digital]] is a *subset* of the non-physical — one way of representing information — which is why "[[physical-vs-digital|physical vs. digital]]" mislabels the real distinction. For co-goods, the non-physical layer is where most of the shareable, compounding value lives.
