# Nonrival

A **nonrival** good can be used by many at once without being used up — a digital file, a broadcast, a piece of knowledge. Nonrivalness is a property of the *units* being shared, distinct from [[antirival-goods|antirivalness]], which is a property of the *system* enabling the sharing.

Nonrivalness lowers the **cost** of sharing (distribution without depletion); it does not by itself raise the **benefit**. As Olleros's example has it, an mp3 is inherently nonrival but only became transformative once embedded in antirival platforms. Full inclusiveness needs both — low-cost sharing (nonrival) *and* high-benefit sharing ([[antirival-goods]]).
