# Physical

Something is **physical** when it is made of matter and occupies space — a garment, a tool, a building, a body. Physical things are bound by where they are and wear with use, which is what makes most physical goods [[rival-goods|rival]] at the unit level: one person's use leaves less for another.

Its true opposite is [[non-physical]], not "digital" — a common slip the [[physical-vs-digital]] comparison untangles. The co-goods move turns on the difference: a product can be physical and rival, while the system around it — designs, knowledge, relationships, participation — is non-physical and can be shared without depletion.
