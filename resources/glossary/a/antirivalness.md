    definitions:
      - The property of a good or system whose value increases as more people use it — the positive extreme of the rivalry axis: use doesn't merely fail to diminish it (as with a nonrival good), it actively improves it.
relevance: A system that grows more valuable as participants join — value compounding for them, not lock-in — the property Co-Goods wraps around rival products.
