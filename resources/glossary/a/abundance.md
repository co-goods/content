# Abundance

A condition in which scarcity no longer governs access — most naturally for [[nonrival-goods]] goods, which can be shared without being used up. The co-goods diagnosis builds on this: as supply becomes abundant, the scarce factor becomes demand, and coordinating it becomes the valuable work.
