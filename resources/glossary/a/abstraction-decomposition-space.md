# Abstraction-decomposition space

The **abstraction-decomposition space** (ADS) comes from Jens Rasmussen's work on cognitive systems engineering and Kim Vicente's Cognitive Work Analysis. It models a domain along two independent axes: an *abstraction* hierarchy that runs by means and ends — from why a system exists (functional purpose) down through its functions to how it is physically realised (physical form) — and a *decomposition* hierarchy that runs by part and whole, from the whole system to its components.

The value is that purpose and mechanism stay connected: any point in the space can be read upward ("what is this for?") or downward ("how is this done?"). Co-Goods uses the ADS as a way to map how a high-level purpose connects, level by level, to the concrete things that realise it — and to let people enter at the altitude that suits them and drill in (see [[progressive-disclosure]]).
