# Platform Scale

## Overview

Sangeet Paul Choudary's *Platform Scale* (2015) is a design-led companion to platform strategy: it treats the **core interaction** as the unit of design and frames scaling as the removal of friction from how platforms *pull, facilitate, and match* participants — powered by [[network-effects|network effects]].

Concrete platform-design mechanics relevant to coordinating participation in co-goods systems.
