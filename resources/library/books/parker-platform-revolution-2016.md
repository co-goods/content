# Platform Revolution: How Networked Markets Are Transforming the Economy

## Overview

Parker, Van Alstyne, and Choudary (2016) give the canonical account of platform businesses: value comes from *orchestrating interactions* between producers and consumers, and the engine is [[network-effects|network effects]] rather than economies of scale. The book covers design, governance, openness, and metrics.

## Notes

The reference text for how platforms aggregate demand and compound through network effects — the dynamics co-goods reworks so that participation accrues to the many rather than the platform owner.
