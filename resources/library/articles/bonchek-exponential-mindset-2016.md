# How to Create an Exponential Mindset

## Overview

Mark Bonchek (2016) contrasts a *linear* mindset — which adds, owns, and controls — with an *exponential* one that multiplies value by leveraging networks and platforms ([[network-effects|network effects]]). The piece is short and framing-level: a vocabulary for the mental shift the network economy demands.
