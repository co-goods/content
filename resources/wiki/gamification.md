# Gamification

## Summary

The use of game-design elements — points, levels, badges, challenges, leaderboards, rewards — in non-game contexts to shape motivation, engagement, and behaviour.

## Overview

Gamification borrows the feedback structures of games to make participation more legible and rewarding. Applied well it can scaffold mastery and belonging; applied badly it substitutes extrinsic rewards for intrinsic motivation, or manipulates. The salient questions are which behaviours get reinforced, and who benefits.

## Relevance to Co-Goods

Bears on the participation and engagement ladder — how to invite and sustain contribution without reducing it to point-scoring.
