# Eight forms of capital

## Summary

A framework from Ethan Roland and Gregory Landua recognising eight distinct kinds of capital — beyond the financial — that flow between people and communities.

## Overview

The framework widens "capital" beyond money. The eight forms are:

- **Financial** — money, securities, and other financial instruments.
- **Material** — physical, non-living resources and infrastructure.
- **Living** — ecosystems, soil, water, plants, and animals.
- **Social** — relationships, networks, trust, and influence.
- **Intellectual** — knowledge, ideas, and information.
- **Experiential** — embodied know-how built through doing.
- **Cultural** — shared meaning, story, and ritual within a community.
- **Spiritual** — connection, faith, and inner growth.

The point is that regenerative enterprises create and exchange value across all eight, not only the financial.
