# Post-scarcity

## Summary

A condition or economy in which some class of goods, services, or information is so abundant — or so cheap to (re)produce — that it's effectively free, and scarcity-based pricing no longer governs it.

## Overview

Post-scarcity describes the breakdown of scarcity as the organising assumption of economics for a given good. It is driven by automation, falling marginal costs, and especially **non-rival** goods — information, designs, software — that can be copied at near-zero cost. The same idea travels under the warmer label **"abundance economy."**

## Relevance to Co-Goods

Post-scarcity is the backdrop for co-goods themselves: non-rival, shareable goods whose value compounds when they are shared rather than withheld.
