# Geoffrey Parker

## Bio

Professor at Dartmouth College and research fellow at the MIT Initiative on the Digital Economy. With Marshall Van Alstyne he helped formalise the economics of two-sided markets, and the two co-authored *Platform Revolution* (2016) with Sangeet Paul Choudary.
