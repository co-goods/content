# Sangeet Paul Choudary

## Bio

Author, advisor, and entrepreneur focused on platform business models and network effects. He founded Platform Thinking Labs, co-authored *Platform Revolution* (2016) with Geoffrey Parker and Marshall Van Alstyne, and wrote *Platform Scale* (2015) and *Reshuffle* (2025).
