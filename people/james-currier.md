# James Currier

## Bio

A four-time founder and managing partner at NFX, an early-stage venture firm. He writes extensively on network effects and marketplace dynamics; NFX's *Network Effects Bible*, a widely cited catalogue of network-effect types, is the firm's best-known piece on the subject.
