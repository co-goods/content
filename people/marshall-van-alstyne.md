# Marshall Van Alstyne

## Bio

Professor at Boston University and research fellow at the MIT Initiative on the Digital Economy. A foundational scholar of two-sided markets and information economics, he co-authored *Platform Revolution* (2016) with Geoffrey Parker and Sangeet Paul Choudary.
